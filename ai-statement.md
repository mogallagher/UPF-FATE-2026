## Statement on AI usage in labs

The use of AI tools is discouraged for practice sessions, but is not prohibited. If you use AI you must disclose the exact purpose it was used for in the declaration section at the bottom of the notebook. The disclosure should be as specific as possible, e.g. *“I did not know how to do operation x, so I used [AI tool] to learn about the library/function y, which I used in [Section] [Question].”*

The following qualifies as acceptible use of AI tools:

* For programming questions, this can include cases such as *"what is the most optimal way to look up rows meeting criteria a and b in pandas"*. The use of StackOverflow is recommended before the use of Generative AI tools to facilitate discussion and deepen understanding of commonly used libraries in data science.


You may not ask Generative AI tools practice questions verbatum. Your final answer to a practice question should primarily reflect your own work. You should be able to explain all of your answers, written and programming. In the case you cannot explain an answer, you will receive a 0 for that question.
